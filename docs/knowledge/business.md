# Business Context — said-wat

> **Purpose:** Why this app exists and how it fits the builder's daily work.
> **Status:** Current
> **Verified against:** Alignment interview 2026-08-09 (source: `docs/records/meetings/2026-08-09_alignment-said-wat.md`)

## Context

The builder communicates daily with English-speaking colleagues (Teams work groups) and reads English web content. Two recurring friction points drove the idea:

1. **Long English messages** (tens of lines) arrive and are tedious to read — the builder wants a hotkey screenshot → instant interpretation, including translation, a one-line summary, and the subtext/ambiguity/action points.
2. **Writing English replies** carries risk of non-idiomatic phrasing or ambiguity — the builder wants drafts (written in English, or expressed as Chinese intent) turned into dependable English, with a check that the reply actually answers what was asked.

## Usage pattern

- The app lives in the background (tray); it only surfaces when triggered by a hotkey.
- Capture source is agnostic: chat apps (Teams), browsers, anything on screen.
- Cost sensitivity: image inputs are cheap; thinking mode is deliberately off; a single provider keeps things simple.

## Success signals

- A long English message can be understood in seconds without reading the whole text.
- An English reply can be drafted and polished in under a minute without worrying about tone or grammar.
